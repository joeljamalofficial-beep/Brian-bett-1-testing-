FAQ

Questions before you join

What exactly do I get after registering?
Membership access and the football content described by the service. Exact delivery details should be confirmed with support before payment.
How long does membership last?
The listed plans currently display a 60-day membership period.
Are football predictions guaranteed?
No. Football predictions are opinions based on available information and analysis. No prediction can guarantee a particular match result.
How do I pay?
Contact support first to receive the applicable payment instructions for your country. Verify the recipient and amount before sending any money.
My country isn't listed. Can I still ask?
Yes. Use the "Request my fee" button and contact support to ask whether your location is supported.
How often are predictions posted?
The service can publish selections according to available fixtures and its matchday schedule.
